Daft Punk are a French electronic duo who rose from 1990s house pioneers to some of dance music’s most iconic names. They blended Chicago house and Detroit techno with pop, funk, indie rock and hip-hop, and later reinvented soft rock and R&B sounds on Discovery. Their live shows helped pave the way for arena-sized electronic music, and Random Access Memories looked to the past to create the future.
